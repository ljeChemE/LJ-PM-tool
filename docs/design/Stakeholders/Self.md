# Self

The one person who uses this tool: capturing tasks throughout the day, and
checking the shape of the week from the dashboard.

## Domain of awareness

- What's due or planned for today.
- Anything slipping (past its planned day, still open).
- The overall shape of the current week — what's done, in progress, not
  started.

## Data this role keeps current

- Every task: its title, status, and which day/week it belongs to.

## Value this role receives

- A place to put a task down in a couple of seconds, so it isn't held in
  working memory.
- An honest, at-a-glance picture of the week, built entirely from tasks
  already captured — no extra bookkeeping required to get it.

## Not expected to

- Categorize, tag, or estimate tasks beyond what's needed to place them on a
  day and a status. Anything heavier than that is friction the guiding
  principle rules out by default.

## Open questions

- Whether a task can span multiple days/weeks, or is always single-day —
  revisit once the daily-list feature forces a real answer.
